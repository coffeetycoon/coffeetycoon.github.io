Welcome to Coffee Tycoon!

How to Play:

Build your coffee empire from the ground up! Click the coffee cup to brew coffee manually, then purchase items from the Shop to automate your production.

Quick Keys:

1 Brew House - Manual brewing
2 Shop - Purchase production items
3 Upgrades - Permanent improvements
4 Lab - Research drink recipes
5 Roastery - Roast beans into blends
6 Prestige - Reset for bonuses
7 Achievements - Track progress & earn rewards
8 Stats - Production graphs & lifetime stats

Super Coffee:

Keep an eye out for ☕ Super Coffees popping up around the screen — click them for temporary bonuses like Espresso Rush (2× CPS) or Click Frenzy! Rare ✨ Golden Super Coffees can be bought with Golden Coffee for even stronger buffs, and mysterious ❓ Mystery Coffee Beans grant rare rewards. Watch the skies for ⛈ coffee storms that boost all CPS 2×–5×!

Goal:

Earn coffee, unlock upgrades progressively, and reach 10 billion coffee to prestige for permanent Golden Coffee bonuses!

Coffee Tycoon v1.19

What's New in v1.19

  • Stackable Roastery Upgrades: Master Roaster, Blend Mastery, and Double Batch can now be bought repeatedly — each stack strengthens the effect and doubles the next price
  • Smarter Auto-Buy: Auto-Buy Items and Auto-Buy Upgrades now buy the single next-cheapest affordable thing each pass
  • Icon indicators: ✅ owned, 🔔 affordable/unclaimed, ◻️ locked — no more [x]/[!]/[] text markers
  • Number shorthand: all visible numbers use k/M/B/T notation (exact values on hover)

What's New in v1.18

  • Stats Window: New 📊 button (or press 8) opens a full statistics dashboard
  • Overview tab: Total coffee, CPS, and Golden Coffee cards plus time played, clicks, prestiges, and max CPS
  • Production tab: Historical CPS and coffee graphs with hover tooltips showing exact numbers
  • Buildings tab: Per-building CPS breakdown with share %, lifetime production, text filter, and bar chart
  • Multipliers tab: Every active multiplier (prestige, drinks, blends, storms...) in stacking order
  • Lifetime tab: Lifetime stats for everything — clicks, offline earnings, beans, storms, and more
